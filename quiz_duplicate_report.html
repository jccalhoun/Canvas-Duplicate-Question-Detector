Bank usage and orphan check Contents ↑

A bank is linked when a quiz has a question group that draws from it, or a New Quiz uses single questions from it. Deleting a linked bank breaks those quizzes. A bank is copied only when no quiz links to it but Classic quiz questions were copied from it; the quizzes hold their own copies. Otherwise it is unused. Usage counts every quiz in the export, including ones left out of the comparison with --exclude-title.

Each bank that is not linked gets a verdict from where its questions also live in the same export. Only quizzes and linked banks count as in use, so two unused copies of one bank do not vouch for each other. For questions that are not in use anywhere, one bank is picked to keep, so the other copies can go: first every bank holding a question no other bank has, then the bank holding the most of the rest, and so on.

A quiz in another course can also draw from a bank, and the export cannot show that. Unpublished quizzes count as using their banks and holding their questions; run with --only-published to leave them out.

Duplicate quizzes and banks Contents ↑

Modified questions Contents ↑

Each group is one question that exists in more than one version. Version A is the most-used version, or the first one found when there is a tie; every other version is shown as a diff against it: added and removed wording, in the stem and in each choice. ✓ marks the credited answer. Groups where the versions disagree on the answer key are listed first: there the question is worded the same and only the choices or the key changed.
